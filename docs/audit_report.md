## TASK 2: IDENTIFY PROBLEMS

Based on the empirical audit of the PyTorch `.pt` datasets, here are the core problems that will implicitly damage the GraphSAGE system during train-time.

1. **Suboptimal Chemical Skew (The MinMaxScaler Problem)**
   - *What is wrong*: We scaled Molecular Weight and TPSA using a `MinMaxScaler` which linearly crushes outliers between 0 and 1. Due to high organic variance in drugs, the data crushed itself against the zero-bound (Mean = 0.025). 
   - *Why it fails GraphSAGE*: GNNs rely on deep linear algebra layers where inputs with extremely microscopic non-centered variances lead directly to the "Vanishing Gradient Problem" (loss locks up instantly).
2. **Dense Zero-Mean Conflict** 
   - *What is wrong*: While dense scalars crave Zero-Mean representation, Textual TF-IDF matrices absolutely *must* remain entirely sparse (Zeroes must stay pure zero).
